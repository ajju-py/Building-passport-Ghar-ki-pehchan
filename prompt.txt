# 1. PROJECT

Project name:

**Building Passport**

Building Passport is a digital identity and lifecycle management platform for buildings.

The concept is similar to a passport for a person, but instead it creates a **digital identity, history, information and condition record for a building**.

The long-term system will contain:

* Building identity
* Building information
* Construction details
* Building plans
* Building photographs
* Structural information
* Builder information
* Owner information
* Inspection records
* Defects
* Repairs
* Maintenance history
* Expected repairs
* Documents
* Building health assessment
* AI-assisted building health/risk prediction

The final product should feel like a **real civil-tech product**, not a college-project website.
